### FLUX status — conflicting signals, need direct API/page check next review

This is the most important open item. Two WebSearch queries returned directly
contradictory answers on whether standard Octopus Flux (as opposed to
Intelligent Octopus Flux) has reopened to new sign-ups:

- One search claimed standard Flux reopened in **late July 2026**, citing a
  specific quote-run detail (a London peak export rate on 24 July).
- Another, equally confident, search claimed **neither** Flux nor Intelligent
  Flux has reopened, citing articles dated as far back as May 2026.

Given this direct contradiction — and given that WebSearch synthesis can
misread or conflate SEO-farm articles that copy each other without
updating — I did **not** act on either claim. `closedToNew: ["FLUX"]` is left
unchanged this month. **This needs to be resolved with a primary source next
review**: either the octopus.energy sign-up page directly, or (better) the
Kraken API's own `available_from`/availability fields on the FLUX product
family, once fetch access is restored. If standard Flux has genuinely
reopened while Intelligent Flux stays paused, the current single `"FLUX"`
regex is too broad and needs splitting once the actual product codes for the
two variants are known (see NEEDS APP-CODE CHANGES below).
